About the college & the programme

Mid‑Valley International College sits in Gyaneshwor, Kathmandu — small enough that faculty know your name by week three. The BIT programme leans hands-on: labs over lecture slides, and a final year that folds into an internship with an actual company, not a simulated one.

Batches stay small, several instructors still work in software or networking on the side, and the placement cell has built relationships with partner companies that feed straight into final-year internships.
